ARC Prize 2026 - ARC-AGI-2 Competition Info
============================================
Verified against arcprize.org on 2026-08-09. Kaggle's own pages are JavaScript-rendered
and could not be read, so every number below comes from arcprize.org, not Kaggle.

Prize: $700,000 USD total
  - Progress Prizes ($275K, by final leaderboard rank):
      1st $75K, 2nd $50K, 3rd $40K, 4th $35K, 5th $25K, 6th $20K, 7th $15K, 8th $15K
  - Grand Prize ($275K): best solution writeup, judged on six criteria, not on score alone
  - Bonus ($150K): first eligible solution scoring 85%+ on the private eval set
      Unclaimed, ARC Prize "intend to roll this forward to 2027" — their word, not a guarantee

Dates:
  - Submissions due: November 2, 2026
  - Papers due: November 8, 2026
  - Results announced: December 4, 2026
  - New entrant deadline: October 26, 2026

Teams: 76 (as of March 28, 2026)
Max team size: 5

Submissions: 1 per day = ~219 submissions total (March 28 - November 2)

Hardware (Kaggle submission environment):
  - GPU: NVIDIA Tesla P100 (16 GB VRAM)
  - Runtime: 12 hours max
  - No internet access
  - Efficiency limit: ~$0.42 per task ($50 budget for 120 tasks)

Scoring:
  - 2 guesses per task, if either matches exactly you score 1
  - Public leaderboard = semi-private eval set
  - Final ranking = private eval set (revealed at end)
  - The 85% bonus is read off that same private scoring pass, there is no separate
    or ongoing evaluation after the Kaggle close

Requirements:
  - All code open sourced under a permissive public domain license (CC0 or MIT-0)
  - Third-party code needs at least a public-sharing license (Apache-2.0, GPLv3)
  - Open sourcing happens BEFORE official private eval scores are handed out
  - Notebook-only submission on Kaggle
  - No external API calls (no GPT, Claude, etc.)
  - Prizes are at the sole discretion of ARC Prize Inc., subject to Technical Team
    review, and they may issue more or fewer awards
