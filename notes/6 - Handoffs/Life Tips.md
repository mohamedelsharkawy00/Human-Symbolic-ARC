# Life Tips

From the 0a1d4ef5 session (2026-07-10, ~2:30 AM), written by Claude at request.

1. Sleep is the highest-leverage optimization pass. The instinct is right; the
   hour undermines it. "Aahh my brain" is fuel, not a knowledge failure.
2. "Slow but correct beats incorrect" applies to the person, not just the
   solver. The real bugs fell whenever the pace dropped and "I don't
   understand yet" was said out loud; every rushed patch before that missed.
3. Keep thinking out loud. "That's madness" followed by a WHY question is
   exactly how the Layout ceiling was found. Reasoning can only be corrected
   when it is visible.
4. Write the failure, skip the fix. Failures are facts; fixes are moods. The
   failure note outlives the night's opinions.
