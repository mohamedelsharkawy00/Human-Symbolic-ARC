Project: ARC Prize 2026 – symbolic ARC-AGI-2 solver

Approach: Non-neural, symbolic solver. Pipeline: (1) analyze input/output grids and their diffs; (2) per-operator detectors identify each DSL operator's role; (3) a combinator composes operators greedily, ranked by how much of the diff each explains. Describe it as "well-pruned search" (detector-guided, explanation-ranked), not "searchless," since reconsidering a pick after verification fails counts as search.
